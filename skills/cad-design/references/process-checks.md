# Process-dispatched manufacturing checks

Before a check, read the part's route, material state, component map, function, critical surfaces, capability profile and version-bound evidence. The handler owns calculations and capability criteria. This reference states obligations without duplicating thresholds.

Dispatch every component/operation/obligation. Include each disconnected physical solid. If identity, profile or check support is missing, report UNKNOWN coverage with a stable finding. Do not choose FDM, invent criteria or pass omitted obligations. Catalogue buys need purchase identity, rating and interface checks. They do not need made-part geometry checks. Outsourced custom routes remain in scope.

Geometric findings describe the measured observation. Process outcomes use declared profile/calibration criteria. The schema and handlers own those rules. Legacy wall, build-volume, overhang, bridge and hole heuristics, plus `block_like`, are diagnostics/proposals only. F0-F3 fidelity is appearance review.
A finding records stable id/code, subject, severity/outcome, measured value/units or UNKNOWN reason, criterion/source, method/evidence, uncertainty, consequence, remedy/alternatives/tradeoffs and recheck scope.
For cut datums, `process.laser.flatness` and `process.plasma.flatness` compare `flatness <=` with the calibrated upper bound. For waterjet edges, `process.waterjet.edge_finish` compares `surface_roughness <=` with calibrated finish. Missing calibration yields UNKNOWN. A calibrated bound above the criterion yields FAIL. Inspect the cut edge to assess its actual quality.

## FDM

Use approved part-local orientation and supplied capability/material conditions. Review bed contact and print stability. Assess structural walls separately from tooth tips, chamfers and decorative details. Check small features and hole axes. Review bridges, overhangs, islands, support start/contact/removal access and layer direction relative to load. Inspect critical bores, seats, journals, gears, cams, press fits, inserts and bosses individually. Consider calibrated shrinkage/warping, hole compensation, elephant foot, seams, stair stepping and finish. Identify needed coupons, slicer review and print trials. B-rep geometry does not test slicer toolpaths or prove a successful print.

Record reachable supports on named noncritical surfaces, including contact and removal access, finishing allowance and inspection. A threshold creates a planning obligation that a credible support or bridge plan may address. Precision/running/contact surfaces need a justified finishing route, tool access and dimensional/surface revalidation. Check removal damage, distortion and supported finish. Soluble/interface support alone does not establish precision. Prefer orientation or relief over splitting because a split creates joints, fasteners, stacks and load paths.

## CNC machining

Check stock identity/form/grade and machinability against evidence. Review axes, setups, datums, fixtures, workholding and clamping distortion. Check cutter/holder access, diameter, reach, radii, deep pockets, slender tools, undercuts and unsupported features. Assess drilling/tapping access, tool sizes, engagement, blind depth and breakthrough. Check thin walls, burrs, finishing allowance and inspection access. Compare tolerance/finish needs with demonstrated capability and the datum/setup plan. Collision-free tool access does not prove machinability or qualification.

## Laser cutting, scoring and engraving

Distinguish through-cut, score and engraving. Engraving does not create precision pockets or bores. Verify stock composition/coating/thickness against the specific laser, power, gas, extraction and approved conditions. Assess capability-backed kerf/compensation, taper, minimum holes/slots/webs, edge quality, heat-affected area, dross/burning, distortion and sheet flatness. Include stock variation, nesting/grain, loose cutouts and micro-tabs. For tab/slot joints include sheet thickness, kerf, coating and clearance; call for representative coupons where needed. Identify secondary deburring, cleaning, coating, drilling/machining and inspection. Assess assembled rigidity, alignment, joints and load paths. Reject unsafe, unidentified or unapproved material. Do not recommend PVC. A laser type does not imply it cuts every metal or polymer.

## Separate sheet processes and other shaping

Waterjet and plasma use separate profiles and criteria for kerf, taper, edge, minimum features, thermal effects and finishing. Do not inherit laser capability. Sheet forming checks stock/thickness/grain, bend radius/allowance/springback, reliefs, hole-to-bend distance, tooling/access/sequence, coating damage, joining access/distortion, folded tolerances and load paths. Resin printing checks orientation, supports at critical surfaces, drainage/venting, trapped resin, cleaning, curing, distortion, stability, ageing and brittleness. Casting/moulding checks draft, wall transitions, shrinkage, parting, tooling access, undercuts, defects and secondary machining, including tooling cost versus quantity. Extrusion and other processes need an explicit profile/check contract. Missing coverage stays UNKNOWN.

## Purchased and multi-operation routes

Verify catalogue identity, dimensions, ratings, material, interface, mounting, retention, availability and limits with supplier evidence. Do not apply made-part wall, overhang or cutter tests. For transparent purchases/stock, verify optical identity/grade, thickness, supplier and interface criteria. Opaque routes cannot meet the hard optical constraint. Do not downgrade transparency or substitute unknown clear plastic.

Assess every operation in a multi-step route and its complete sequence. Include material/state transitions, datum transfer, stock and finishing allowance, work/tool access, joining, coating, post-processing and inspection. An operation or output file does not stand for the route. Missing operation profile means coverage UNKNOWN.
