# Starter material profiles

These 17 sourced profiles provide typical starting points for comparison and early design decisions. They are not design allowables, approved material/process combinations, or evidence of physical performance. Each typed value carries its source URL, document/revision, access date, test condition, exact quote and reported source statistic; an open-ended specification or qualitative result is never rounded or converted into a fabricated point or interval. Research unknowns stay explicit in profile guidance; profile notes retain unrepresentable entries, source limitations, and conversion or reporting details.

To use a profile, copy it into the project record at `hw/manufacturing/materials.yaml`. A copied profile does not qualify the delivered grade, product form, lot, process, print or heat-treatment state, orientation, conditioning, or service conditions. A project must still establish the purchased grade and condition, resolve unknowns relevant to its use case, and obtain or derive appropriate design allowables and verification evidence. Typical values and standard minimums are not allowables.

Caveats recorded in the profiles include third-party-hosted ISO standards previews, incomplete Fiberlogy TDS extraction, and polycarbonate laser-cut edge discoloration/char behavior. The PC laser source addresses edge quality, not occupational safety approval; local machine rules and the material SDS govern. The cited acrylic laser guidance is not approval for a specific machine or workplace. Transparent feedstock or a transparent-looking print does not establish optical transmission; for transparency, source a purchased transparent part or identified transparent stock with traceable purchase criteria, as required by the project decision.
