# Manufacturing-intent record

The version-2 `hw/` record owns service, constraints, candidate selection, per-part route intent, capability and evidence. See `layout.md` for canonical paths and `layout-api.md` for path APIs. Schemas and `manufacturing_intent.py` own fields and interpretation. Missing and legacy facts remain unknown. They never mean zero, PASS or a default process/material.

## Use case before selection

`product.yaml` stores the use case, release scope and evidence policy. During GROUND, ask about purpose, users, service class, environment, loads, motion, duty/life, accuracy/finish/stability, failure consequence, safety/standards, quantity/cost/time, maintenance/repair, equipment/operators/outsourcing and release scope. `requirements.yaml` stores distinct requirements, preferences, assumptions and open questions. Keep sources, bounds, owners and question status. Do not infer service or material from CAD geometry.

Selection compares full architecture/material/route combinations in `manufacturing/selection.yaml`. Each mandatory evaluation has an outcome, criterion/source and method. FAIL eliminates the candidate. UNKNOWN stays conditional. Preferences rank only candidates that pass every mandatory requirement. Include secondary operations, finishing, joining, inspection, quantity-specific estimates, maintenance and tradeoffs. `selection.py` owns deterministic comparison; it reports proposals and does not approve or change design.
Run the read-only comparison with `uv run --script "$CAD_SKILLS/cad-flow/scripts/run.py" cad-record/scripts/selection.py <project|hw> [--json] [--out .cad-work/<report>.json]`. Markdown is the default; JSON may be written only under `.cad-work/`. Exit 0 means the use case and every decision permit a final selection; exit 1 means conditional, blocked or not assessed; exit 2 means invalid record or infrastructure/input error. The report includes the canonical `input_sha256`, missing use-case facts and questions, hard-constraint evaluations, each decision's feasible/conditional/eliminated candidates, comparison, optical purchase criteria, recommendation and findings. The command is report-only: it never updates the project.

To record an assessment, copy the report's digest into `selection.yaml` `assessment.input_sha256`; set the assessed state/date/method. A confirmed final choice additionally records the approving `decision`, the selected primary route, and a final recommendation supported by the assessment. Re-run after any input in the digest scope changes; do not recalculate or hand-author the digest. Unknown material/process selection is not an empty-but-acceptable choice: a design freeze or release with no recorded selection is blocked, just like any other unknown. A candidate assessment is not itself approval.
The starter catalog at `skills/cad-record/assets/manufacturing/material-profiles/` supplies sourced, grade/form/condition-scoped typical values, always marked `typical_not_allowable`. It is reference data, not a project selection or capability qualification. Copy a profile by stable ID only when its exact grade and conditions match; qualify the actual project material and derive any allowable separately. Unrepresented properties remain unknown. Capability templates are guidance only; project capability must come from actual equipment or supplier evidence and scoped calibration.

An optical requirement is a hard constraint. Opaque routes are ineligible. Consider a traceable purchased transparent part or identified transparent stock on a suitable evidenced route. Include identity/grade, optical grade, thickness, supplier evidence and interface criteria. Do not recommend see-through FDM or unidentified clear plastic.

## Version-2 record contract

The one project version is `product.yaml: record_version: 2`; absence means readable v1, and unsupported explicit versions refuse. V2 requires `product.use_case`, requirements lists, per-part `intent`, interface functional contracts and top-level `assembly.operations` (which is the sole authority; v2 `drawing.operations` is rejected). A fact is known only with source and basis, or explicitly unknown/not applicable with a reason. Known estimates include uncertainty; assumed facts cite a bounded assumption; allowables include conditions and derivation. Supporting source files are hash-bound under `hw/manufacturing/sources/` or part sources. Process and material profiles have independent ids and remain separate from requirements.

The use case records status/decision, service class, purpose/users, temperature and environment, loads, motion/duty/life, accuracy/finish/stability, failure consequences/severity/standards/supervision/authority, quantity/budget/lead time/maintenance/repair, actual capability/operator/outsourcing, release purpose/limits/exclusions, and evidence policy. Requirements retain acceptance criteria and may scope subjects, conditions, risk, evidence level and waiver policy. `preferences[]`, `assumptions[]` and `open_questions[]` are distinct; resolved questions need a resolution source or decision.

Part `intent` contains required `functions`, `material_profile` (profile id, unknown or not applicable), `service` (requirements/loads/environment/duty/life/differences), `critical_surfaces` and ordered `route`. Operations record process, capability, provider, components/targets/dependencies, input/output and material states, stock/datums/workholding/access, orientation/approval, support strategy, allowances, post-processing, inspection, criteria and evidence. Catalogue operations hold purchase identity/specification/ratings/interfaces; they are not made-part checks. Components bind disconnected geometry once by interior witness or semantic locator, with optional per-component profile/function/multiplicity. Interface `functional` records criticality, functions, loads/environment/surfaces/acceptance, tolerance variation case/contributors/method, material contact conditions, play/compliance, assembly operations and inspection/decisions.

Capability profiles describe the actual machine, process/provider, parameters, stock/material approvals, tools/workholding/operators and calibration scopes/results/sources/validity. Resolution or general-tolerance labels alone are not calibration. Material profiles identify family versus grade, form and process/conditioning/orientation state, sourced property values and conditions, and whether values are typical-only. Starter material profiles live in `assets/manufacturing/material-profiles/`; all are `typical_not_allowable`. Capability templates contain unknown guidance only. Neither catalog is a selected project profile.

Selection `candidates[]` compares whole architectures and per-part material profiles/routes/capabilities, purchase alternatives, mandatory requirement evaluations, cost/lead time, maintenance, evidence, obligations, pros/cons/tradeoffs and preference scores. The report-only `selection.py` digest covers use case, requirements, interfaces, relevant part fields, capability/material profiles and selection candidates, but not its own assessment/recommendation. A confirmed selection carries that digest, assessment method/date, recommendation and approving decision. Transparent/translucent function or part property creates hard `optical.route` and `optical.transmission` obligations: opaque routes are eliminated; an alternative must identify a purchased transparent item or suitable identified transparent stock with grade/optical evidence, supplier and interface criteria.
The transparent FDM polycarbonate profile describes feedstock only. It does not establish optical transmission.

## Part intent and capability

Each part's top-level `intent` declares functions, service and differences, material profile or unknown, route or unknown, critical surfaces, components and rationale. Existing make/buy and manufacturing-state fields retain their authority. Ordered route operations name process, capability, material state, setup, orientation, supports, allowances, finishing, inspection, criteria and evidence. Missing facts remain unknown. Catalogue purchases use sourced identity, dimensions, ratings, supplier evidence, availability and interfaces. They receive no made-part checks. An outsourced custom part still requires a real route and capability review.

`manufacturing/capabilities.yaml` describes equipment, work envelope, tooling, stock approvals, operators, workholding, measurement and scoped calibration. It describes the supplied shop or vendor; it does not set product requirements. Machine resolution and general tolerance are descriptive, not calibration. Capability templates provide fields and guidance. `manufacturing/materials.yaml` records family/grade, form, process state, orientation, conditioning, temperature, property sources and conditions. It distinguishes typical properties from justified allowables. A property without matching evidence stays unknown. Density does not yield strength or authorize another material's values.

Bind each disconnected component exactly once. Group repeats only when geometry, material, route, setup and critical context agree. Use stable semantic/native-frame locators. Do not use persistent face indices or shape hashes as identity. Assess every operation in a multistep route, including datum transfer, allowances and access. Unsupported processes receive UNKNOWN coverage, never an FDM substitute.

## Fits, assembly and evidence
`assembly_operations` finding codes include `assembly.predecessor_unknown`, `.order_impossible`, `.closed_before_install`, `.into_absent`, `.installed_twice`, `.part_absent`, `.retention_unknown`, `.engagement_unknown`, `.torque_unknown`, `.joint_temperature`, `.joint_temperature_unknown`, `.edge_distance`, `.edge_distance_unknown`. Access adds `assembly.part_path_blocked`, `.tool_blocked`, `.access_unknown`. Play adds `motion.play_collision`, `.play_clearance`, `.play_unknown`. Stack-up findings are `stack.worst_case_violation`, `.statistical_violation`, `.rss_refused`, `.contributor_unknown`, `.effect_missing`, `.limits_unqualified`, `.designation_not_capability`, `.capability_unresolved`, `.state_mismatch`, `.record_mismatch`.
`access.py` checks a declared `assembly.operations[].path` leg by leg in the receiver's frame. Each path point is the operated part's offset from its installed pose; its orientation stays fixed. Without a path, the tool checks the default straight insertion along the recorded direction from the separating plane. A named tool needs a cited solid envelope and usable approach; a name alone is not geometric access evidence. Parts already present in each order/closure state are obstacles, except the receiver, fitted partners and operated part. Results are nominal-pose sampled geometric evidence, not an assembly trial; unknown geometry or any unproven interval stays UNKNOWN.

For joining operations, `assembly_operations.check_joints` returns `assembly.joint@op:<id>` outcomes and findings for sourced retention, engagement, torque, edge distance and relevant host-temperature compatibility. Fasten/insert/heat_set require a declared edge-distance criterion and distance; a violation is critical. These declared facts do not calculate joint strength, pull-out, creep, fatigue, preload or cure. They need qualified source evidence or an appropriate physical test.

Interfaces may declare play as total motion about the nominal centred pose and compliance as additional elastic movement under stated loads. `play.py` checks nominal plus the corners of the declared play box by sweeping geometry; undeclared relevant play, unknown joints or load without declared compliance remain unknown. `scenario_cases` can select recorded motion cases. Corners and sampled poses do not cover intermediate offsets, other directions, nonlinear contact or unrecorded compliance; no dynamics are simulated.

For each critical interface, record function, requirement, contacting materials, endpoints, datum, fit, dimension state, finish, conditions and verification. Separate desired tolerance from demonstrated capability. Separate nominal from worst-case remaining clearance. Use worst-case stacks when collision, binding or lost function matters. RSS requires justified distributions, correlation assumptions, coverage and approval. Include thermal, moisture, coating, adhesive, joining, deformation and wear when relevant.

Normalize assembly operations from the assembly record. Assess installation order and closure state. Check insertion/removal paths, tool approach/reach, engagement, retention, torque, adjustment, inspection, service and disassembly. Tool names alone do not show access. Run a geometric access check only when its inputs and method support that claim. Otherwise report UNKNOWN.

Evidence levels are scoped facets: `cad_valid`, `geometrically_manufacturable`, `process_planned`, `simulation_supported`, `physically_validated`. Bind each outcome to its subject, criterion/source, method, dependencies, uncertainty and coverage. Geometry results describe only their measured claim. A preview, heuristic, envelope fit, motion sample, material name, analysis result or plan does not prove physical process success, strength, accuracy, life or regulatory compliance. Static FEA does not prove fatigue, creep, wear or life.

Findings are immutable producer results with stable code, subject, severity, outcome and measured value with units or unknown reason. Each names criterion/source, method/evidence, uncertainty, consequence, remedy/alternatives/tradeoffs and recheck scope/stage. `findings.yaml` contains dispositions anchored to exact producer evidence. A disposition does not turn UNKNOWN or FAIL into PASS. Waivers remain waived.

## Legacy migration and gates

Version-1 records remain readable with explicit unknown manufacturing intent. Run `uv run --script "$CAD_SKILLS/cad-flow/scripts/run.py" cad-record/scripts/migrate.py <project|hw> [--dry-run|--apply] [--json]`; dry-run is the default and shows exact per-file changes, questions and findings without writing. The project owner may explicitly use `--apply` to change mutable design records and add a dated migration note. Migration is idempotent, validates changes, writes `product.yaml` last, and refuses unsupported versions, invalid input/results or a frozen record without an open change. It never edits models, geometry, sealed versions/releases or evidence bundles, and never invents evidence. Version-1 records cannot start gated actions or new record-writing builds, freeze or release before migration. Historical sealed formats remain inspectable in their original form.

A new freeze/release requires a recorded material/process selection and cannot treat missing selection, intent, unsupported route, required UNKNOWN, stale evidence, incomplete service policy or unqualified waiver as PASS. An approved safe trial waiver may retain a precisely scoped unresolved obligation where policy allows. It remains unvalidated and cannot waive safety, regulatory or geometry failures. See `evidence.md` and `cad-flow/references/manufacturing-selection.md`.
Migration exit codes are 0 for current or applied, 1 for pending dry-run changes, and 2 when refused. The dry-run plan reports paths, before/after changes, summary, questions, unknown facts and findings. `--apply` preserves comments/formatting, verifies parsed results and schemas, writes a dated `hw/docs/record-migration-v2-<date>.md` note, then writes product.yaml last for crash-safe reruns. It refuses unsupported versions, invalid input/result schemas or a frozen record without an approved open `flow.change`. It edits only mutable records: no model.py, geometry, sealed snapshots/releases/bundles, decisions, BOM or verification; migration approves nothing.

New freeze records `flow.frozen.evidence_sha256` and its approving decision. Release manifest v3 seals acceptance inventory, service scope/limits, open obligations/waivers, claims by evidence level, per-part routes and component package files. Historical release manifests v1/v2 remain inspectable, not re-certified.
